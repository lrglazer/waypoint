My story

I couldn't get a straight answer.

As an engineering student, my whole schedule hangs on prerequisites. Miss one class in the right semester and you can lose a year. So I needed my advisor: to clear holds, confirm my transfer credit counted, and check that my plan actually worked.

What I got was emails that went unanswered, follow-ups that never happened, and holds that sat on my account while registration got closer. I started keeping track of every time I didn't hear back. Eventually I had to take it above my advisor just to get things moving.

The frustrating part: most of what I needed wasn't a judgment call. It was reading a catalog, checking a transcript, and doing the prerequisite math. A computer can do that in seconds. That's when I decided to build it.

The point

Not replacing advisors. Freeing them.

Let AI handle the routine work: degree audits, prerequisite checks, catalog questions, simple holds. Then advisors can spend their time on the conversations that actually need a human. Waypoint even writes your meeting notes so that one short meeting counts.
